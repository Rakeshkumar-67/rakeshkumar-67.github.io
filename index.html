About Me

CFD and thermal-fluid engineer with 7+ years of simulation experience across turbomachinery, rotorcraft, and renewable energy systems.

I specialize in high-fidelity transient CFD analysis, development of User-Defined Functions (UDFs) for complex kinematics, and HPC-based parallel simulations. Currently, I work as an Assistant Manager – R&D at Metal Power Analytical Pvt Ltd, where I contribute to the design, thermal management, and performance optimization of Optical Emission Spectrometers (OES) and other analytical instruments — applying CAE, CFD, FEA, and thermal-fluid simulation to real-world product development. Prior to this, as a Postdoctoral Fellow at IIT Bombay, I focused on the aerodynamic optimization of cyclorotors and tandem rotor systems.
